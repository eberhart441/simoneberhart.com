I made a small foundry out of an oil barrel, ceramic wool, refractory cement, and a weed burner. It gets hot enough to melt bronze, at around 950 °C for the alloy I used. I mostly built it because I wanted to try casting strange shapes. So far that's meant city blocks and a couple of rings.

1Furnace

The barrel is lined with ceramic wool and refractory cement, with the burner entering near the bottom. Once it's up to temperature I can put a crucible of bronze inside and melt it for pouring. Getting the metal molten was easier than making molds that produced decent castings.

2Cityscapes

The city sculptures started as 3D geometry from Google Maps. I used RenderDoc to capture the mesh while the scene was being rendered, then wrote Python scripts to clean it up. The geometry was a mess: holes, overlapping triangles, and detail too fine to survive a casting. I reduced the mesh, closed the surfaces so it could be treated as a solid, and made two-part molds from the resulting models. Then I poured the bronze. It's a somewhat roundabout way to make a tiny piece of a city, but I like the result.
