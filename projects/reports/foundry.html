A furnace I built in my backyard out of an oil barrel, ceramic wool, refractory cement and a weed burner. It melts bronze, which takes about 950 °C. I used it to cast sculptures of city blocks and a couple of bronze rings.

1The furnace

It's an oil barrel lined with ceramic wool and refractory cement, with a weed burner pointed into the bottom of it. Light it and the inside gets hot enough to melt bronze.

2The sculptures

The sculptures are chunks of real city. Google Maps has 3D models of the buildings, so I used RenderDoc to capture the geometry as it draws them. The raw mesh is full of holes and way more detailed than a cast can hold, so I wrote some Python to simplify it and close it up into a solid. Then I made two-part molds and poured.

3Rings

I cast two bronze rings with it too.
