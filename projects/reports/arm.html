This is a robot arm drive with one motor. Clutches decide which joint the motor moves, so a single motor can run the whole arm. It's been my main side project for over two years and it's still a work in progress. I don't have performance numbers yet. I plan to raise money for it at some point.

1The idea

Most robot arms have a motor in every joint. This one has a single motor. Every joint has a clutch, and the clutch decides whether that joint gets driven or sits still. To move one joint you engage its clutch. To move another you let go and engage a different one.

The bet is that fewer motors makes for a simpler arm. The hard part is the clutches, because everything depends on them engaging and letting go cleanly.

2Three versions

I've built the clutches three ways. The first used servos to engage them. The second used solenoids I designed myself. The third uses off-the-shelf solenoids. That work led to a few provisional patents.

4Where it's going

I started out building this as its own platform, a whole arm with its own control software. Now I'm moving it onto Universal Robots instead. It becomes an end effector that goes on a UR arm, so I don't have to build the whole arm.

The plan is to raise money for it at some point. Right now there are no performance numbers, and I'd rather say that than make some up.
