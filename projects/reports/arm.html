One motor runs the whole arm. Clutches pick which joint it moves. It's been my main side project for a bit over two years and it's still a work in progress. No performance numbers yet. I'm planning to raise money for it at some point.

1How it works

Most robot arms stick a motor in every joint. Mine has one motor and every joint gets a clutch. Engage a clutch and that joint moves. Let go, engage a different one, and the motor moves that joint instead.

Fewer motors should mean a simpler arm. The tricky part is the clutches, since the whole thing depends on them grabbing and letting go cleanly.

2Three versions

I've done the clutches three ways. First with servos to engage them. Then with solenoids I designed myself. Now with off-the-shelf solenoids. A few provisional patents came out of all that.
