A filament winder I built to go with a Filabot EX2 extruder. It makes recycled filament come out a steady width. I designed the whole thing, from the frame to the motor control, and put it on Printables, where it's been downloaded over 150 times.

1How it works

A magnet and a hall-effect sensor measure the width of the filament. It runs between them and pushes the sensor away from the magnet, and the hall sensor reads how far. An Arduino watches that number and speeds the winder up or slows it down to keep the width steady. Pull faster and the filament gets thinner. Pull slower and it gets thicker.

It holds the width to around ±0.05 mm.

2The winder

I designed all of it, the frame, the motor control and everything between. It's 3D printed.
